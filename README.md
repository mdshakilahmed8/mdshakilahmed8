### 🚀 What I'm building

**[ChatAllTime](https://chatalltime.com)**: an AI sales and support assistant for Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and websites. It answers customer questions, quotes real prices from the store catalog, takes orders and captures leads 24/7, and hands the chat to a human when needed.
- Multi-channel inbox with live agent handoff
- RAG over each business's own catalog and FAQs
- Store sync for Shopify, WooCommerce and custom APIs
- Next.js 16, TypeScript, MongoDB, Redis/BullMQ, Qdrant, OpenAI-compatible LLMs and Claude, shipped with Docker and zero-touch CI/CD

**Multi-tenant e-commerce SaaS**: storefront, admin and APIs for online sellers.

At **ZincLoop**, where I'm Founder & CEO, I lead product engineering, cloud architecture and delivery for clients. Previously a partner at Barakah IT.
